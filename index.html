Get Involved

Ways to plug in

Volunteer, apply for a role, or bring Fair Trade practices to your own group.

We open Director, VP, and Presidential positions every May. Volunteers are the backbone of JSEC — they make our events both sustainable and impactful.

  • Subscribe to the newsletter to be notified the moment applications open.
  • Volunteer roles run year-round across Events, Marketing, and Projects.

Concordia has been a designated Fair Trade Campus since 2016. All campus coffee is Fairtrade-certified (excluding certain franchises), and a multi-stakeholder Fair Trade Steering Committee keeps pushing that commitment further.

  • Look for the Fair Trade mark on your next coffee, tea, chocolate, or banana.
  • Encourage your student group to adopt Fair Trade purchasing policies.
  • Follow the conversation on Instagram @cufairtrade.

We pair students with professionals working in sustainability, so you can build a real network before you graduate — not after.

  • Open to students at any year or program.
  • Matches are made based on interest area and career goals.
